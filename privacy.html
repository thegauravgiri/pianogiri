Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Your music stays on your device

When you turn audio into a lesson, the file (or microphone recording) is decoded and analysed entirely inside your browser using the open-source Basic Pitch model. The audio is never uploaded to our servers. The AI model files are downloaded from a public CDN (jsDelivr) the first time you convert a song.

Recording from a YouTube tab

When you make a lesson from a YouTube video, your browser records the sound of the YouTube tab you choose to share, while it plays. The recording is processed and stored only in your browser. PianoGiri asks YouTube for the video's title (via YouTube's public oEmbed service) to name your lesson.

What we store

PianoGiri has no accounts. Your progress (XP, level, streak, best scores, badges), your settings and the songs you create are saved in your browser's local storage, and the original audio of songs you import is kept in your browser's IndexedDB so it can play along with you. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which keeps PianoGiri free. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts (typefaces)
  • cdnjs and jsDelivr (Tone.js, the MIDI library and the Basic Pitch model)
  • tonejs.github.io (piano sound samples)
  • Google AdSense (ads)

These services receive your IP address when your browser downloads files from them.

Copyright

Only convert recordings you have the right to use. Lessons you create stay in your own browser and are not published by PianoGiri.

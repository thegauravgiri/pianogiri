How PianoGiri works

  1. Pick a song or upload audio / MIDI. Audio is turned into notes right in your browser by an AI model (Basic Pitch) — your file never leaves your device.
  2. Choose how to play. Practice waits for you at every note. Perform keeps the beat and judges your timing. Watch plays it for you first.
  3. Hit, hold, combo. Short tiles are taps, long tiles must be held until the end. Chain hits for a multiplier up to ×4.
